RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Residential Rental Agreement ("Agreement") is made on 1 April 2026 between
Meridian Properties Private Limited, a company incorporated under the Companies
Act, 2013, having its registered office at 14 Brigade Road, Bengaluru 560001
("Landlord"), and Ms. Anita Raghavan, resident of 22 Palm Grove, Bengaluru
("Tenant").

1. PREMISES AND TERM

1.1 The Landlord lets to the Tenant the residential premises at Flat 7B, Lakeview
Residency, Bengaluru 560103 ("Premises") for a term of eleven (11) months
commencing on 1 May 2026 and expiring on 31 March 2027.

1.2 The Tenant shall use the Premises solely as a private dwelling and shall not
carry on any trade, business or profession at the Premises without the prior
written consent of the Landlord.

2. RENT AND DEPOSIT

2.1 The Tenant shall pay monthly rent of Rupees Forty-Five Thousand (INR 45,000)
in advance on or before the fifth day of each calendar month, without demand and
without deduction or set-off of any kind.

2.2 Any rent remaining unpaid after the due date shall attract interest at the
rate of three per cent (3%) per month, compounded monthly, from the due date
until the date of actual payment.

2.3 The Tenant has paid an interest-free security deposit of Rupees Five Lakh
(INR 500,000), which the Landlord shall refund within ninety (90) days after the
Tenant vacates the Premises, after deducting all sums due under this Agreement.

3. ESCALATION AND RENEWAL

3.1 This Agreement shall renew automatically for successive periods of eleven
(11) months unless either party gives written notice of its intention not to
renew at least sixty (60) days before the expiry of the then-current term.

3.2 On each renewal the monthly rent shall increase by ten per cent (10%) over
the rent payable during the immediately preceding term.

4. TERMINATION

4.1 The Landlord may terminate this Agreement at any time by giving the Tenant
fifteen (15) days written notice, without assigning any reason.

4.2 The Tenant may terminate this Agreement only after completion of six (6)
months of the term and only by giving the Landlord three (3) months written
notice, failing which the Tenant shall forfeit the entire security deposit.

5. MAINTENANCE AND ALTERATIONS

5.1 The Tenant shall at the Tenant's own cost keep the interior of the Premises,
including all fixtures, fittings, plumbing and electrical installations, in good
and tenantable repair, fair wear and tear excepted.

5.2 The Tenant shall not make any structural alteration or addition to the
Premises without the prior written consent of the Landlord, which consent may be
withheld at the Landlord's absolute discretion.

6. INDEMNITY AND LIABILITY

6.1 The Tenant shall indemnify and keep indemnified the Landlord against all
claims, demands, actions, proceedings, losses, damages, costs and expenses of
whatever nature arising directly or indirectly out of the Tenant's occupation or
use of the Premises, whether or not caused by the Tenant's negligence.

6.2 The Landlord shall not be liable to the Tenant for any loss of or damage to
the Tenant's property, or for any personal injury sustained at the Premises,
howsoever caused, and the Tenant waives all claims in that regard.

7. ENTRY AND INSPECTION

7.1 The Landlord or the Landlord's authorised agent may enter the Premises at any
reasonable time for the purpose of inspection or repair, after giving the Tenant
twenty-four (24) hours notice, which notice may be given orally.

8. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

8.1 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be
referred to and finally resolved by arbitration by a sole arbitrator appointed by
the Landlord, and the seat of arbitration shall be Bengaluru.

8.2 The Tenant waives any right to commence or participate in any class,
collective or representative proceeding against the Landlord.

9. GOVERNING LAW

9.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws
of India, and the courts at Bengaluru shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
